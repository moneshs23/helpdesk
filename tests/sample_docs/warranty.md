# AcmeCool 3000 Air Conditioner — Support Handbook

## Warranty
The AcmeCool 3000 comes with a **5-year manufacturer warranty** on the compressor
and a **2-year warranty** on all other parts. The warranty covers manufacturing
defects but does not cover damage from improper installation.

## Price
The recommended retail price of the AcmeCool 3000 is **$1,299 USD**. Authorized
dealers may offer seasonal discounts.

## Installation
Installation must be performed by a certified technician. The unit requires a
dedicated 20-amp circuit. Improper installation voids the warranty.

## Maintenance
Clean or replace the air filter every 3 months. Schedule a professional
inspection once per year to keep the warranty valid.

## Return Policy
Unopened units may be returned within **30 days** of purchase for a full refund.
A 15% restocking fee applies to opened units.

## Safety
Always disconnect power before servicing. Do not operate the unit if the power
cord is damaged.
